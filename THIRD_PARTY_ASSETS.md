## DualSense exterior

**PS5 Controller** by **Taohid Animation**, [original model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ps5-controller-b7bb9c5102a04cb0b1966c6d02bad7d6), [creator](https://sketchfab.com/taohidanimation), **CC BY 4.0**. Author-platform API confirmed the downloadable flag and CC Attribution / BY 4.0 license on 2026-10-19.

The semantically separated GLB is mirrored from [SafaElmali/dualsense-studio](https://github.com/SafaElmali/dualsense-studio/blob/main/controller/dualsense.glb). Original credit and modifications are also embedded in the GLB. Geometry, UVs and textures are retained; the lab applies a uniform display scale, rotates the coordinate frame, remaps selectable assemblies, adjusts materials and supplies independently authored internal hardware. The exterior is a visual model, not factory CAD or evidence of the internal layout.

Files: public/models/dualsense/dualsense.glb and NOTICE.txt. License: [CC BY 4.0](public/licenses/CC-BY-4.0.txt).

## Double Gauss prescription

Kramer Harrison, [Optiland DoubleGauss sample](https://github.com/optiland/optiland/blob/master/optiland/samples/objectives.py), **MIT**. Radius, thickness, glass sequence, stop position and rear image distance are used without dimensional rescaling. Original optical data copyright/license is retained in [optiland-MIT.txt](public/licenses/optiland-MIT.txt). The mechanical barrel, section window, retainers, iris mechanism and adapters are independently authored teaching geometry. Their dimensions are not from the optical sample. Clear radii are visualization choices and not a complete validated aperture prescription.

SCHOTT Sellmeier coefficients come from the refractiveindex.info database copy shipped in Optiland 0.6.3, **CC0**: [notice](public/licenses/refractiveindex-CC0.txt). No bundled Python dependency is needed by the web app.
